docling-pdf reads the glyph names of PDF fonts through data that comes from
Adobe: the Adobe Glyph List in this directory (glyphlist.txt), and tables
compiled into the crate that are derived from the ITC Zapf Dingbats Glyph
List and from the Adobe Core 14 AFM files (src/symbol_fonts.rs: the Symbol
and ZapfDingbats encodings, advance widths and glyph names; src/std14.rs:
the advance widths of the twelve text faces). Their notices follow.
